The archives

Notes, write-ups and slides

What people wrote down at past WhereCamps. Unconferences leave few records, so every item here is worth keeping. No video recordings have turned up yet. Links marked archived go to a copy on the Internet Archive because the original page is gone.

Know of something that is missing?

If you have session notes, photos, slides or a recording from a past camp, add an entry to archives.json and open a pull request. Each entry needs the camp name, a title, a type and a link.
